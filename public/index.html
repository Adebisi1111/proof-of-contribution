How consensus works

1

Independent fetch

The leader rates your artifact. Each validator separately fetches the same URL and re-rates it from scratch.

2

Exact match required

Rating is an integer 1–5. Validators agree only on an exact match — no fuzzy band for a rigged leader to hide in.

3

Commit or nothing

On disagreement the round records nothing at all. A leader inflating a score is rejected, not discounted.
